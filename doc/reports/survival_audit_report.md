| 95% | 41 | 115 | 26.3% |
| 100% | 41 | 115 | 26.3% |

### Assessment

Without explicit censoring-type indicators, we cannot distinguish:
1. Programs still active (administrative censoring)
2. Programs terminated/cancelled (competing risk)
3. Programs past deadline (administrative censoring)

If termination is correlated with capacity, the independent censoring assumption is violated.
**Recommendation**: Document this limitation. A Fine-Gray model would require termination status data.
