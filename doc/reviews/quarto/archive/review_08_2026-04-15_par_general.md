Recommends either theoretical/statistical/decision-analytic grounding (via simulation) or recast as heuristic reporting convention.

3. **QCEW framing should be narrower** — evidence shows *NAICS 925110 proxy* fragility, not "administrative capacity in general." Separate proxy fragility from construct reassignment (financial-ratio bridge involves both).
4. **Sample construction opaque** — Appendix A.5 approximate counts ("~2,400 / ~500 / ~100 / ~150") unacceptable for a leading journal; exact inclusion/exclusion accounting needed; consider local-only as main analytical population.
5. **Cross-framework triangulation overinterpreted** — SEM-vs-Cox varies too many dimensions; bring within-Cox divergence (time-varying null vs baseline positive) to main text; narrow claim.
6. **Paper straddles methods vs empirical identities** — choose more decisively; prefers methods-forward framing with bounded CDBG-DR demonstration.
7. **Practice recommendations overreach** — AdminResources "benchmark-ready" labeled coefficient is not policy-ready due to portfolio-complexity caveat; EfP should emphasize methodological lessons first, policy targeting second.
