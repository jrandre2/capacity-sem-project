## Review Summary

R9 is the highest-level critique to date. Where R6 proposed the audit-protocol reframe, R7 asked for operationalization (decision rule, estimand taxonomy), and R8 stress-tested whether the resulting normative superstructure held up, **R9 argues that the superstructure should be partly retracted**: the pass/fail "benchmark-ready" verdict should be demoted to a descriptive dashboard; the reference SEM should be demoted from headline status; QCEW suppression should be treated as a first-order measurement problem rather than one audit item among six.

## Major Comments (8)

1. **Decision rule too ad hoc; baseline-contamination.** 5/6 and 50% cutoffs are author-defined; 50% magnitude is anchored to a reference β the paper itself argues is distorted by suppression, pooling, and maturity. Remove pass/fail or recast as descriptive dashboard.
2. **QCEW suppression undermines the reference, not just the audit.** Treat as first-order measurement problem: missing/censored handling, bounds, or restrict to observable nonzero population. Pooled ε-coded SEM should not be the reference.
3. **Timeliness selected on completion; headline SEM rests on structurally biased sample.** Reverse the paper's hierarchy: censoring-aware or fixed-horizon primary, cross-sectional SEM secondary.
4. **SEM latent model not persuasive enough.** Observed-composite ≈ SEM means latent adds limited leverage. Make observed-variable primary or simplify SEM to exploratory decomposition.
5. **Pooling state+local conceptually difficult.** Local-only similar coefficients do not establish the pooled latent construct is meaningful. Narrow the interpretation or make local-only primary.
6. **Cross-framework = illustrative not adjudicative.** SEM-vs-Cox changes too many dimensions simultaneously; the within-Cox divergence between time-varying and single-spell shows the problem is within-framework too.
7. **Reproducibility claims need sharper limits.** Deposit the 573-case crosswalk directly. Distinguish fully vs. partly reproducible components.
8. **Overlong, presentation.** Abstract appears twice; notation problems in §6.2, Appendix C.7.
