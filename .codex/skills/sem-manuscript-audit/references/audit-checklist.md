# SEM Audit Checklist

## Model Comparison

- Do table values and prose agree on χ², CFI, TLI, RMSEA, AIC, and BIC?
- Is the preferred model chosen by a stated rule rather than hand-waving?
- Are labels possibly swapped between models?

## Measurement Model

- Are all factor loadings reported?
- Are standard errors or uncertainty estimates reported?
- Are residuals, latent correlations, or construct-validity checks reported?
- Is respecification logic documented?

## Structural Claims

- Do moderation claims correspond to interactions, multi-group SEM, or invariance testing?
- Do causal verbs overstate an associative design?
- Do reported path coefficients match the table?

## Design And Timing

- Is the unit of analysis stable and explicitly defined?
- Are pooled units actually comparable?
- Do outcome definitions induce censoring or maturity bias?
- Are year ranges and sample sizes consistent across sections?

## Proxy And Source Validity

- Is QCEW defended as a proxy rather than treated as direct measurement?
- Is SVI harmonization across vintages explained?
- Is matching logic validated and sourced from official files when possible?

## Interpretation And Presentation

- Do captions match panel content?
- Are LOWESS or predicted-value plots mislabeled as PDPs?
- Are outlier rules and robustness samples reported precisely?
- Does the manuscript read as a standalone paper rather than as commentary on revisions, repository state, or audit history?
- Are limitations framed in article-facing terms instead of `we revised`, `the repo lacks`, or `the audit showed` language?
